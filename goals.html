My goals

Oct. 16 2026

To present myself as an ICT student, i have realized a SWOT analysis that will help me understand clearly my objectives and my way of pursuing them. To start, I consider my greatest strengths both my determination to finish things as soon as I start working on them, and the fact I focus on the small details. Next, my biggest weakness is that I get easily distracted, usually by my cell phone, and tend to procrastinate frequently. My opportunities are here at Middelburg, with the ICT programme and the job opportunities it can lead me to. And finally, my biggest threat might be myself; as I mentioned, I tend to procrastinate a lot, and that is something I will work on during my studies.
