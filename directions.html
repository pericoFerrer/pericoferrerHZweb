My directions

Oct. 16, 2026

In this section of my personal blog, I will comment the directions I want to take in the future. For now, the most attractive option for me is to focus on the Data Science branch, because it appeals to me most, but i don’t rule out switching, and I would like to work in any type of work related to music and/or its industry.

Looking at the future, in four or more years, I'll be working in a data-driven role—either as an engineer or a data scientist—in a business that's somehow related to my interests, such as music or audiovisual content, applying my knowledge of data science to, for example, algorithms, data analysis, or machine learning.

As I mentioned in my first blog post, in Europe, there is a wide variety of career opportunities, including positions at record labels, technology companies, digital analytics firms, and audio production companies, among others.
