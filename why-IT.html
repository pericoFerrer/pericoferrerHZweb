Why did I choose ICT?

Since I was very young, I was introduced to technology, and I think I've become a bit dependent
on technology in my life, for better or for worse, so I thought I’d dedicate my life to using that technology
I’ve always used, and applying it to something I love and enjoy, in this case, music, since it has been
the best thing that happened to me this last years, helping me meet people I consider very close nowadays
and to grow as a person, mainly thanks to starting to play an instrument.

About the topics presented in the ICT programme so far, I only have a general idea of what the programme
will cover, but that means I will have to work hard to learn and understand the contents of the programme.
Based on what I know, I think I will choose the Data Science track, since I think it will be the most
useful for me in the future, based in my personal interests.
