About me

Who am I?

My name is Perico Ferrer. I am 18 years old, and I came to Middelburg from Mallorca, Spain.
I used to live for my whole life in a city about the same size as Middelburg, but it has a much lower population.
I ended up in Middelburg on the recommendation of friends who had studied in the Netherlands, and I ultimately
chose HZ over other universities.


My hobbies are playing drums, and I casually play games like Minecraft, or
Valorant, for example. I love listening to music, especially the rap scene from my country, Spain.
Some of my favorite artists I can recommend to get a glance of the Spanish scene are rappers Yung Beef
or collective Disobey, and my favorite bands are Deftones and Spanish-based Extremoduro.

As a worker, I am not as productive as I would like to be, given that I tend to procrastinate,
and one of my goals is to improve my productivity.
